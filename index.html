ProTech LMS Notes

A private, single-user integration that keeps its owner's own IBEW Local 26 / ProTech Skills Institute coursework notes organised in a shared Google Docs tree.

It is not a public product and has no users other than its owner.

What it is

ProTech LMS Notes reads the owner's own coursework from their learning-management system and writes tidy, structured notes and practice documents into their own Google Drive. It runs on the owner's own server and acts only on the one Google account that authorised it.
