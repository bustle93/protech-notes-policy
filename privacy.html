Privacy Notice

ProTech LMS Notes is a private integration used by a single person to keep their own coursework notes organised in Google Docs. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive and Google Docs — to create and update the owner's own notes and practice-test documents, and to read the folders those documents live in.
  • Google Sheets — to read and write the owner's own study and practice data.
  • Google Calendar — to read the owner's own calendar.
  • Gmail — to read and send email on the owner's behalf.

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. Nothing is sold, shared, published or transferred to any third party. The integration runs on the owner's own server and stores only its own OAuth credentials and the documents it is asked to create. No data is sent to any external analytics, marketing or advertising service.

Data retention and removal

Everything the integration creates lives in the owner's own Google account and can be deleted there at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all access.
